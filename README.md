# 🌱 WattWise Campus

**🚀 Live Demo:** [Open WattWise Campus](https://watt-wise-campus.vercel.app/)

A smart campus energy management dashboard that helps users estimate electricity consumption, explore potential savings, and identify appliances with high energy usage. This platform designed to help students and institutions understand electricity consumption and identify opportunities to save energy.

## Problem Statement

Electricity is often wasted in campuses because people lack clear, accessible information about appliance energy consumption and its cost.

## Proposed Solution

WattWise Campus aims to make energy usage easier to understand through a simple web interface and practical energy-saving insights.

## Planned Features

- Appliance electricity consumption calculator
- Estimated daily and monthly electricity costs
- Energy-saving scenario simulator
- Practical energy-saving recommendations
- Dashboard summarizing usage and potential savings
