
# WattWise Campus — HackCult '26 Pitch

## Slide 1 — The Problem
- Electricity waste can happen when appliances are left running unnecessarily.
- Students often lack a simple way to estimate appliance energy costs.
- Campuses need accessible tools to encourage energy-conscious habits.

## Slide 2 — Our Solution
- WattWise Campus is a web-based energy-awareness tool.
- Users enter appliance power, quantity, usage hours, and electricity tariff.
- The app aims to show estimated consumption, cost, and potential savings.

## Slide 3 — Product Demo
Demonstrate the following after implementation:
1. Open the dashboard.
2. Enter appliance details.
3. View daily and monthly energy estimates.
4. Reduce usage hours in the savings simulator.
5. View the updated estimates and recommendations.

## Slide 4 — Technical Architecture
- Frontend: React and JavaScript
- Development server and build tooling: Vite
- Calculation logic: JavaScript module
- Initial version: client-side processing without a backend
- Include an architecture diagram and data flow.

## Slide 5 — Testing and Impact
- Show verified calculation test results.
- Demonstrate input validation.
- Explain estimated cost and energy savings.
- State assumptions clearly; do not present estimates as measured campus-wide savings.

## Slide 6 — Future Scope
- Campus-level energy-saving challenges
- Sustainability awareness and gamification
- More appliance profiles and usage comparisons
- Possible integration with real energy-meter data in a future version
